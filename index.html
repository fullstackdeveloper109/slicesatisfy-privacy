Slice Satisfy – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile game Slice Satisfy (package name com.slicesatisfy.game, "the App"), made by Slice Satisfy ("we", "us"), handles information when you use it. By using the App you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, or precise location.

Game progress stored on your device. The App saves your level progress, star ratings, settings, and whether you have removed ads. This data is stored only on your device and is never sent to us. It is deleted when you uninstall the App or clear its data.

Advertising (Google AdMob). The App shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google may collect and process information such as:

  • your device's advertising ID (Android Advertising ID)
  • your IP address, from which an approximate location may be inferred
  • device information such as model, operating system version, language and screen size
  • information about how you interact with ads, such as views and taps

Depending on your choices and where you live, these ads may be personalized. Google's use of this data is governed by Google's own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services
  • AdMob and user data

App diagnostics (Unity). The App is built with the Unity game engine. Unity may collect anonymous technical data, such as device type, operating system, performance information and crash reports, to help keep the App stable. See the Unity Privacy Policy.

2. How information is used

  • to run the game and remember your progress (on your device only)
  • to show ads, including rewarded ads you choose to watch, which help keep the App free
  • to find and fix crashes and performance problems

We do not sell your personal information.

3. Consent and your choices

  • Consent. Where required by law (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), the App shows a consent form from Google before showing personalized ads. You can choose whether to allow personalized ads.
  • Advertising ID. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, at any time in your device settings under Settings > Privacy > Ads (the exact path may vary by device).
  • Deleting your data. Uninstalling the App deletes all game data stored on your device. Because we do not keep any data about you on our own servers, there is nothing else for us to delete. For data collected by Google, see the Google links above.

4. Data sharing

We only share data with the third-party services described above (Google AdMob and Unity), which act under their own privacy policies. We may also disclose information if required by law.

5. Security

Information sent between the App and these services is encrypted in transit. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but these providers use industry-standard safeguards.

6. Children's privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us and we will help remove it.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
